# Arizen Polymers — client content checklist

Everything below is still needed from Arizen. Until it arrives, the website shows a clearly
marked **"To be provided by Arizen"** placeholder in its place. Nothing has been invented.
At launch, switch off **Settings → Launch readiness → Show placeholders** to hide any
sections that are still empty.

Tick items off as they are entered in the dashboard (`/admin`).

## Brand

| ☐ | Item | Format | Where it goes in the dashboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Official logo | SVG or transparent PNG/WebP, horizontal and square versions | Settings → Company → Official logo |
| ☐ | Brand guidelines (colours, fonts, logo usage) | PDF | Shared with Neureonix to confirm the website palette |
| ☐ | Social media links (LinkedIn, etc.) | URLs | Settings → Social links |

|---|---|---|---|
